## LISTA FECHADA

**Gramática do tópico:** I/you/we/they + verbo na forma do dicionário (I work, you live, we like,
they play) · negativa: I/you/we/they don't (do not) + verbo · contraste: to be + not × don't + verbo.

**Verbos (8, da versão anterior):** work · live · like · play · study · eat · drink · sleep
**Palavras de apoio:** in Brazil · in technology · video games · English · coffee · tea · breakfast ·
at home · every day · well · bread · music · and · but · not · I/you/we/they · my · a/the · very ·
+ tudo do T9 (to be, I'm not, tired, student, developer, friends, brother…)

**Proibido:** he/she/it com verbo normal (works, -s); doesn't; "Do you…?"; -ing (reading, playing);
números por extenso (eight, seven), horas (at seven), dias da semana (on Sundays).
⚠️ Versão anterior tinha: "I like playing video games", "I like reading" (ck6_1), "at seven",
"eight hours", "on Sundays" → **saem**.
